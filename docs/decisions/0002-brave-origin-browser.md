# 0002 – Brave Origin as the browser

- **Status:** accepted
- **Context:** The browser needs a reliable app-window mode and built-in ad blocking, and must be free on Linux.
- **Decision:**
  - Brave Origin, with Chromium's mature app-window mode and Shields ad blocking.
  - Browser extensions are **out of scope**: installed by hand, if wanted.
- **Rejected:** Firefox. Its kiosk mode is hard to get right.
- **Accepted trade-off:** streaming sites cap their quality on Linux.
